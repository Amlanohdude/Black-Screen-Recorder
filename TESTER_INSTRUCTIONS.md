# Closed Testing Instructions — Black Screen Recorder Pro

Welcome to the closed test of **Black Screen Recorder Pro** (`com.blackscreenrecorder`). Thank you for helping us test and improve the app!

---

## 📋 Prerequisites
1. You must have an Android phone running **Android 7.0 (Nougat) or higher** (API 24+).
2. Your Google Account email address must be registered and added to the tester list by the developer.

---

## 🚀 How to Download & Install

### Step 1: Accept the Tester Invitation
1. Open the testing opt-in link on your phone or computer:
   👉 **[Join on the Web](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.blackscreenrecorder)**
2. Make sure you are signed in with the **same Google account** provided for the test.
3. Click the **"Become a tester"** button.
4. You should see a confirmation message: *"You are now a tester"*.

---

### Step 2: Download from Google Play Store
After becoming a tester:
- **Direct Link:** Tap **[Download it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.blackscreenrecorder)**
- Or, on your Android phone, open the **Google Play Store**, search for your accepted tests or click the download link from the opt-in page.
- Tap **Install**.

---

## 🧪 What to Test

Please test the following key features during your daily usage:

1. **Permissions Setup:**
   - Grant required permissions: **Camera**, **Microphone**, and **Notifications**.
   - Check if the permission prompts appear clearly.

2. **Recording & Discreet Display:**
   - Start a video/audio recording.
   - Test the dark / OLED black screen mode while recording.
   - Verify that the app maintains smooth recording in the background via the persistent notification.

3. **Storage & Playback:**
   - Check saved recordings list.
   - Test renaming, playing, sharing, and deleting recordings.

4. **Stability & Performance:**
   - Keep the app running during longer recording sessions.
   - Note any excessive battery drain, overheating, crashes, or glitches.

---

## ⚠️ Important Note: 14-Day Testing Requirement
Google requires closed testers to keep the app installed and actively engage with it for at least **14 consecutive days**. 
- Please **keep the app installed** on your device.
- Open and use the app every few days to ensure Google recognizes active testing engagement.

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting & FAQs

- **"App not available" or "Item not found" (Error 404):**
  - **Account Mismatch:** You might be signed into a different Google account in your browser or Play Store app. Switch to the exact email address you provided for the closed test.
  - **Propagation delay:** Google Play can take 1–3 hours after a release is published to propagate to all testing accounts. Please try again shortly.
  - **Opt-in not completed:** Make sure you clicked *"Become a tester"* on the web link before opening the Play Store link.

- **How do I provide feedback or report a bug?**
  - You can submit private feedback directly on Google Play Store on the app listing page (only the developer will see it).
  - Or contact the developer directly with screenshots, device model, and Android OS version.
